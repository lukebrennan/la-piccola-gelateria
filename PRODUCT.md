# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Angular (standalone components, signals, SCSS), static build deployed to GitHub Pages from a repo sub-path first (`username.github.io/la-piccola-gelateria/`); a purchased custom domain will be added later, so nothing may hard-code the base path. Firebase (Auth + Firestore) is planned for a later backend phase: a private owner login to see orders. Payments will be mocked.

## Users

Tourists and visitors already in or planning a trip to Amalfi, often on their phones while walking, who want to know what gelato is available, when the shop is open, where it is, and how to get there. Secondary audience: reviewers of the creator's web design and development portfolio, who judge design, UX, front-end craft, SEO and content strategy.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and ordering site for La Piccola Gelateria, a fictional artisan gelateria on the Amalfi Coast. The site exists as a premium portfolio project. Its conversion goal is to get visitors to physically visit the shop, by making it easy to see the flavours, check opening times, find the shop and get directions. A mock ordering flow (no real payments) and a private owner dashboard are phased in after the frontend.

## Positioning

An independent, small-batch gelateria made by the sea, with Amalfi lemons and ceramic-tile heritage as its signature, not a themed Italian restaurant template.

## Operating Context

Fictional business. All addresses, hours, contact details and stories are invented: Via dei Limoni 18, 84011 Amalfi SA, Italy; open every day 10:00 to midnight. Sample flavours: Limone di Amalfi, Pistacchio, Stracciatella, Fragola, Cioccolato Fondente, Fior di Latte; menu categories Classici, Frutta, Specialità, Senza Latte.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Pages and URLs: `/` home, `/gelato/`, `/our-story/`, `/ingredients/`, `/visit-amalfi/`, `/journal/`, `/journal/<slug>/` (three articles: amalfi-lemons, gelato-vs-ice-cream, flavours-of-amalfi-coast). Interactive gelato menu with category filtering. Strong technical and local SEO: unique titles and meta descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph, LocalBusiness / IceCreamShop, Breadcrumb and Article structured data, semantic single-H1 pages, optimised responsive images, lazy loading, font loading, mobile performance. Static hosting means no server-side rendering. Primary keyword: gelato Amalfi. Undecided: final custom domain, real photography beyond stock.

## Brand Commitments

Name: La Piccola Gelateria, tagline "Un piccolo assaggio di felicità. A little taste of happiness." Palette: Mediterranean Navy #123B66, Amalfi Cobalt #155CA2, Lemon Yellow #FFD84D, Warm Cream #FFF9EC, White #FFFFFF, Muted Leaf Green #577A50; predominantly cream, white and Mediterranean blue, yellow as accent only. Headings in Bodoni Moda (or similar Italian high-contrast serif), body in DM Sans (brief overrides the kit's EB Garamond / Noto Sans suggestion). Hand-painted, slightly imperfect majolica ceramic motifs used as accents, never giant repeating patterns. Voice: short, warm, confident, occasionally Italian, never cliché. Banned feels: corporate, ultra-minimalist, generic luxury beige, sterile, cartoonish, childish, heavily vintage, template-like, over-lemoned.

## Evidence on Hand

Brand kit v3 at `public/brand/` (SVG logos in five colourways, 13 icons, 12 ceramic artworks) plus favicons and web manifest; product mockups as WebP in `public/images/mockups/`. No real photography, testimonials, reviews or press exist; stock photography from Unsplash is to be used and must be credited. Do not invent reviews, awards or customer numbers.

## Product Principles

1. The goal is a visit: flavours, hours, location and directions are never more than a tap away.
2. Mobile first: most real visitors are walking around Amalfi on a phone.
3. Photography and ceramic carry the story; copy stays short.
4. Ceramic decoration is an accent on a clean modern layout, never wallpaper.
5. Motion is subtle and never makes anyone wait.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Strong colour contrast, obvious clickable elements, keyboard and screen-reader support, honest alt text, reduced-motion respected. Dietary information (milk-free, nuts) is shown on flavours.
