Our story

A little family counter.
A lifetime of Italian summers.

Made with amore

Small batches.
Big Italian summers.

It began with a seafront cart and a notebook of family recipes. Today it is one small counter on Via dei Limoni, a few steady hands, and the same rule as ever: make it fresh, make it simple, make it slowly.

Our recipes are short on purpose. Fruit in season, milk from nearby farms, nuts from the best groves we can find. A handful of ingredients, a little patience, and nothing to hide.

“Taste it warm. Taste it twice.
Never hurry the churn.”
