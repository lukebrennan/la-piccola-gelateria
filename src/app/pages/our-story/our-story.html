A small counter with a long memory.

Three generations of recipes, one narrow street in Amalfi and a rule we have never broken: make it fresh, make it simple, make it slowly.

It started with a cart.

Long before there was a shop, there was a small painted cart on the seafront and a notebook of family recipes. Summers on the Amalfi Coast taught us two things: people want something cold and honest, and the best flavours are the ones that taste of where you are.

The cart became a counter on Via dei Limoni. The notebook is still on the shelf, a little stained with lemon, and we still check it before every new flavour.

A notebook, not a manual.

Our recipes are short on purpose. Most fit on half a page: a handful of ingredients, a temperature and a note in the margin. The margin is where the good advice lives.

Fruit in season, milk from nearby farms, nuts from the best groves we can find. We would rather make six perfect flavours than twenty average ones, and the notebook agrees.
