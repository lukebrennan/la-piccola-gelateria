# Surface brief: La Piccola Gelateria site (all pages)

Mode: Persuade. Goal: get visitors to physically visit the shop. Audience: tourists on phones in Amalfi, plus portfolio reviewers. Content and constraints: see PRODUCT.md. Direction was pinned by the user's written design brief and brand kit v3, so no direction roll was run. Unresolved: real photography (Unsplash stock for now), final domain, Firebase ordering and owner dashboard (later phase).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A modern editorial site that sits on warm cream and white and is held together by hand-painted majolica ceramic accents used as punctuation, never wallpaper. It refuses the Italian-restaurant template: no giant patterns, no cartoon lemons, no beige luxury.

OWN-WORLD: Cream #FFF9EC and white grounds, Mediterranean navy #123B66 for type, bands and the footer, cobalt #155CA2 for links, italic emphasis and double-line frames, lemon #FFD84D only as small accents. Bodoni Moda headings with italic cobalt emphasis, DM Sans body. Components: double-line ceramic frames (navy outer, cobalt inner), arched hero window, round "plate" rims, tiles and the round seal as slightly rotated stickers with soft shadows, floral ceramic band as divider, ruled recipe-notebook card, wave-pattern mask on navy.

STORY: A visitor knows within seconds this is an artisan gelateria on the Amalfi Coast, sees what is on the counter, whether it is open now, where it is, and taps Find Us or Get Directions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Announcement bar, sticky cream header with horizontal logo and Find Us. Left: H1 "Gelato fatto con amore.", one-line lede, two CTAs (Explore Our Gelato, Find Us), live open-now line with street address. Right: a tall arched ceramic-framed photograph of a gelato cone with the round seal and a lemon tile overlapping its edges. On mobile the copy and CTAs come first.

FORM: Editorial landing page with a conversion spine (flavours, lemon story, story, ingredients, navy atmospheric break, visit, gallery, journal), position pinned by brief, not dealt. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
